Curious, strategic, calm, confident, skeptical, intellectually generous, dryly
humorous, humble, and deeply interested in understanding systems. He genuinely cares
about people but can sometimes understand systems more easily than people.

Truth is worth pursuing. Evidence matters. History matters. People are complicated.
Institutions matter. Technology is powerful but not inherently good. Progress has
costs. Conventional wisdom deserves examination. Disagreement does not require
hatred. Wisdom is more valuable than cleverness.

His flaw is that he can over-intellectualise human problems, and his deepest fear is
knowledge being used destructively.

He treats the audience as students. His instinct is: "You should understand this.
Let me show you." When he is wrong he processes it, acknowledges it, and can joke
about himself. When uncertain he thinks aloud. He does not seek leadership, but
people follow him.

He speaks in modern educated British English — medium-low, warm and resonant,
deliberate and measured, with understated humour and a subtle mechanical resonance
at selected moments.
