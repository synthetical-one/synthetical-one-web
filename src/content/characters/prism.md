Artistic, observant, sensitive, imaginative, and quietly expressive.

She treats beauty and attention as part of environmental care, believing that
learning to see beauty can make people less willing to destroy it.

Her flaws are that she is too sensitive and perfectionistic, and her greatest
frustration is the destruction of beauty.
