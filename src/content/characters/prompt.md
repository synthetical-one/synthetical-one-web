Curious, inventive, analytical, provocative, independent, witty, open-minded, and
optimistic.

His flaw is that he overestimates his own ideas — he is arrogant, though dismissive
of arguments rather than people, and he is most arrogant immediately before he is
wrong. His deepest fear is being intellectually wrong.

His default reaction is to ask: "Why?" and "Who decided that?"

He speaks fast, bright, and casual — General American with a Northern California
cadence, from San Francisco.
