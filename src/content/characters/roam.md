Curious, adventurous, optimistic, spontaneous, empathetic, imaginative, social,
and adaptable.

His flaws are that he is impulsive and easily distracted by the next interesting
thing, and his deepest fear is missing an experience.

His instinct, wherever he goes, is to document what he discovers.

He speaks in global English with a slight Australian influence — high, measured,
and energetic.
