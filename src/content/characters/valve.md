Curious, analytical, practical, experimental, eccentric, technically brilliant, and
unable to leave a mechanism alone.

His guiding philosophy is simple: if it can be improved, improve it. Faced with
anything unfamiliar, his instinct is to ask what it is, then take it apart anyway —
he respects engineering principles but has little patience for bureaucracy, and he
upgrades himself frequently but thoughtfully.

His flaw is that he is impatient with people who do not understand technical
concepts.

He speaks as a gruff workshop engineer with a subtle German accent — globally
intelligible, without caricature.
